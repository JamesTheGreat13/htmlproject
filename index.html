About the College of Human Ecology

The College of Human Ecology (CHE) of Nueva Vizcaya State University is based at the Bayombong Campus. Its roots can be traced to the Department of Home Technology, which formed part of the pioneering College of Agriculture when Nueva Vizcaya Agricultural College became the Nueva Vizcaya State Institute of Technology in 1973. The department initially offered the Bachelor of Science in Home Technology.

Over the years, the college's academic programs developed in response to changing educational and industry needs. In 2006, following the conversion of NVSIT into Nueva Vizcaya State University, the college was given its present name, College of Human Ecology. Its programs and curricula continued to evolve, including the development of hospitality, tourism, home technology, food, and nutrition-related studies.

Today, the College of Human Ecology has two departments: the Home Technology Department and the Hospitality Management Department. The college offers the Bachelor of Science in Hospitality Management, Bachelor of Science in Tourism Management, and Bachelor of Science in Home Technology (major in Food and Nutrition). Its graduate offering is the Master of Science in Home Technology, with majors in Home Technology and Home Technology Education.
